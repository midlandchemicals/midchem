About us

A sedation and anaesthesia team for each list

We provide a consultant anaesthetist and an operating department practitioner for a day or half-day session. We work under our own procedures, with our own monitoring and emergency equipment, and keep a bank of clinicians to cover absence.

Founded by a consultant paediatric anaesthetist, a hospital dentist and a general dental practitioner.

  • Individual staff to work under another provider's procedures.

Monitoring

Continuous monitoring for every patient

Each patient is monitored from the start of sedation until they are ready to leave. Where a clinic does not have full monitoring, we bring our own equipment and check it before the day.

  • ECGHeart rate and rhythm
  • SpO₂Oxygen saturation, beat by beat
  • NIBPBlood pressure, cycled automatically
  • CapnographyBreath-by-breath carbon dioxide, which shows changes in breathing early

How it works

From booking to invoice

Step 1 / 5

Step 1 of 5

Four weeks ahead

You book the day. We assign the team and confirm the site checklist: equipment, oxygen, defibrillator and recovery area.

Step 2 of 5

One week ahead

Our anaesthetist reviews each patient's health questionnaire and confirms whether the setting is suitable for them.

Step 3 of 5

On the day

Equipment and emergency drugs checked. A safety checklist before every patient. We sedate or anaesthetise, monitor throughout, and sign each patient off as safe to go home.

Step 4 of 5

Same day

Records are completed and any incidents are reported.

Step 5 of 5

Month end

One monthly invoice, and an updated audit of every patient, covering complications, cancellations and patient satisfaction.
