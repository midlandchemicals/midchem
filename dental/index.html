Your visit

Designed to feel nothing like a clinic.

From the lounge to the chair, every step was rebuilt around one question: what would make this hour genuinely pleasant?

Inside the studio
Step 1 — Arrive

Walk into a lounge, not a waiting room

Warm light, good coffee, no clipboard. Your forms were done from your phone before you left home.

Step 2 — Scan

See your own smile in 3D

A 60-second intraoral scan puts your teeth on screen. We walk through them together — you'll spot things before we say them.

Step 3 — Plan

A fixed price before anything happens

Every option, every cost, in writing, before treatment. Take it home and think — there's never pressure to decide in the chair.

Step 4 — Treat

Noise-cancelling calm

Headphones with your own playlist, a ceiling screen, numbing that actually works, and a hand-signal that pauses everything instantly.
